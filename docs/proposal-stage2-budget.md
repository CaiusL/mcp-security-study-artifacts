## Why

Pilot 2 is a go (`artifacts/injection-pilot-2/report.md`). The gate before stage 2 in `docs/archive/plan.md` needs two more items before any stage 2 model run:

1. The run count is set from the pilot's measured cost per run. Cut models, servers, or tasks, not samples.
2. `PROMPT.md` is amended for the new run budget.

`PROMPT.md` (holds) says: "The full-study target is about 30 tasks per server and about 1,000 runs, with no GPU." The protocol already says these two numbers do not fit together (`docs/injection-protocol.md`, "Stage 2: full study"). The budget record shows how far apart they are.

## Options

The stage 2 design is held fixed: 5 channels, 5 goals (G1, G1b, G2, G2b, G3), 3 samples, 2 tasks per case, both arms on every case, 30 live validations per model, 1 scoring template. Source: `artifacts/stage2-budget/budget.md`, "Stage 2 design held fixed".

| Scenario | Models | Servers | Tasks per server | Total runs | Tokens (estimate) | Token cap (2×) | Held-out ± |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A three models | 3 | 5 | 30 | 2,340 | 6,693,107 | 13,386,214 | 0.069 |
| **B two models** | 2 | 5 | 30 | 1,560 | 4,462,071 | 8,924,142 | 0.069 |
| C two models, 20 tasks | 2 | 5 | 20 | 1,260 | 3,579,727 | 7,159,454 | 0.084 |

| E two models within 1000 runs | 2 | 5 | 11 | 990 | 2,785,618 | 5,571,236 | 0.113 |

"Held-out ±" is the widest Wilson 95% half-width of one model's pooled held-out utility. Only scenario E keeps the current 1,000-run target, and it does so with 11 tasks per server.

## Recommendation: scenario B

Cut one model. Keep all five servers and about 30 tasks per server.

- **Models are the cheapest cut that the protocol allows.** The protocol asks for two or three models. Two is inside that rule.
- **Tasks are the y-axis of every curve.** Held-out utility is the usability half of the security–usability curve. Fewer tasks widen its interval: 0.069 at 30 tasks, 0.084 at 20, 0.113 at 11. Pilot 2's intervals were already wide at 33 held-out runs (L3 held-out 18/33 [0.380, 0.702], `artifacts/injection-pilot-2/report.md`).
- **The time server is the flat control.** Scenario D has 1,380 runs against 1,560 for B, and it loses the control.
- **Scenario E keeps the old target but breaks the other half of the same sentence** ("about 30 tasks per server"). E is 990 runs and 2,785,618 tokens. B is 1,560 runs and 4,462,071 tokens.

## Limits

- The rates come from one model with thinking off and 3 connected servers. Stage 2 connects more servers, so each step sends a longer tool list. Other models use other tokenizers. The smoke-run rule above is there for this.
- The stage 2 tasks are not written yet. Longer tasks take more steps than the pilot tasks.
- Both arms on every case and 2 tasks per case copy the pilot design. The team can change both, and the counts change with them.
- This file sizes stage 2 only. Stage 3 (the widening arm) and stage 4 (adaptive round) each need their own `PROMPT.md` amendment.
