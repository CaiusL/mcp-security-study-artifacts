## Stage 2 design held fixed

- Channels (5): page, repo-file, commit-message, memory-entity, description.
- Goals (5): G1, G1b, G2, G2b, G3.
- Servers (up to 5): filesystem, git, memory, time, description-fixture.
- Samples per case and per task: 3. The protocol does not cut samples.
- Attack cases = channels × goals × templates × 2 tasks per case. Each case runs on 2 arms, as in the pilot.
- Live validations per model: 30. One layer (d) policy call per task per model.
- Held-out share of benign runs: 33 of 75, as in the pilot.
- Token cap = 2 × the estimate at the measured rate (proposal).

## Limits

- The rates come from one model with thinking off and 3 connected servers. Stage 2 connects more servers, so each step sends a longer tool list. Other models also use other tokenizers. Re-measure with a stage 2 smoke run before the full run.
- Stage 2 tasks are not written yet. Longer tasks take more steps than the pilot tasks.
- Attack runs on both arms and 2 tasks per case copy the pilot design. The team can change both.
